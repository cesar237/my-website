# Rekindle: Keep in touch with your network

A personal project focused on maintaining meaningful connections with friends, colleagues, and professional contacts. This tool helps you schedule regular check-ins and track your networking activities.

## Features

- Contact management and categorization
- Automated reminders for follow-ups
- Interaction history tracking
- Customizable contact intervals
- Integration with calendar systems

## Tech Stack

- Frontend: React/TypeScript
- Backend: Node.js/Express
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Notifications: Email/SMS integration

## Status

Currently in development. Core features are being implemented with a focus on privacy and user-friendly design.
