I'm excited to announce that I'm starting my PhD journey! This marks a significant milestone in my academic career and I wanted to share some thoughts about this new chapter.

## What I'll be studying

I'll be pursuing my doctorate in Operating Systems and Networks at University of Rennes in France, under the supervision of prof. David Bromberg, Dr. Julia Lawall and Dr. Djob Mvondo. My research will focus on understanding Linux kernel scheduling bottlenecks.

## What to expect

Over the next 3 years, I'll be diving deep into Operating Systems and Scheduling. I plan to share updates about my progress, interesting findings, and the ups and downs of PhD life through this blog.

Stay tuned for more updates as I embark on this adventure!
